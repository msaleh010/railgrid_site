Deployment & hosting

Run it where your policy says it must run.

Same platform, three hosting models. Because ERPNext is open source and the data is yours, you can move between them later without re‑implementing.

Recommended

Frappe Cloud

Managed hosting by Frappe, the makers of ERPNext. Automated backups, version upgrades, monitoring, scaling and SSL – with RailGrid as your implementation and support partner on top.

  • Go live in weeks with no server team
  • Daily offsite backups, point‑in‑time restore
  • Region selectable for data residency
  • RailGrid managed support included
For regulated data

Private cloud in Tanzania

A dedicated, single‑tenant instance in an in‑country data centre, operated by RailGrid under a service‑level agreement. Built for public institutions and financial services with residency obligations.

  • Data held in Tanzania
  • Encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Named support engineer
  • Patch windows agreed with you
For remote sites

On‑premise

Installed on your own servers at a mine, depot or head office where connectivity cannot be relied upon. The same software, full source access and no lock‑in.

  • Keeps working through outages
  • Your IT team trained to operate it
  • Optional replication to cloud
  • Hardware sizing by RailGrid

What "managed" means in practice.

Whichever model you choose, RailGrid remains accountable for the system working – not just for installing it.

Monitoring

Uptime, performance and backup checks with alerts to the RailGrid support desk.

Statutory updates

PAYE, NSSF, VAT and other rule changes applied and tested before they take effect.

Upgrades

ERPNext version upgrades planned, tested on a staging copy and applied in an agreed window.

Helpdesk

Named contacts, ticket tracking and response targets under a service‑level agreement.
