Core capabilities

From gate‑in to gate‑out, every event has a financial consequence.

Depot operations are modelled as ERPNext documents with their own workflows, and each one links to the customer, the charge and the invoice.

Gate & yard operations

Gate‑in and gate‑out with QR‑token validation, yard positions by block, row and slot, moves and dwell tracking per container.

Manifest & documentation

Bills of lading, manifests and authority documents held in a registry with digital hash, linked to every release.

Tariff & charge linking

Storage, handling, lift and ancillary tariffs applied automatically from operational events – no charge left unlinked.

Invoicing & collections

TRA VFD‑compliant invoices, customer statements, payment verification before release, credit control by agent.

Agent portal

Clearing agents see their containers, charges and documents, request releases and pay online – the "agent super‑app", delivered on the ERPNext portal.

Revenue assurance

Daily reconciliation of operational events to charges to ledger, with variance reports that show exactly where revenue would have leaked.

Control environment

Operations, financials and audit – in one governed surface.

The depot manager, the finance controller and the internal auditor look at the same records, each through their own role.
