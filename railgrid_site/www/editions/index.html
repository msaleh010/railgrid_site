Government & Public Sector

Public Institution Edition

For ministries, agencies, authorities, local government and parastatals that must show every shilling was committed, approved and spent in line with a budget and a delegation of authority.

  • Budget commitment control on requisitions and purchase orders, by vote and cost centre.
  • Procurement workflows with tender records, evaluation and contract management.
  • Establishment control: approved positions, vacancies and payroll linked to the structure.
  • Asset registers with custodians, verification exercises and disposals.
  • Grant and project accounting for donor‑funded programmes with separate reporting.
  • Approval audit that internal audit and oversight bodies can inspect directly.
Banking & Financial Services

Back‑office Edition

The core banking system runs the accounts. Everything around it – people, procurement, assets, branch costs and management reporting – runs here, consolidated and audited.

  • HR and payroll for head office and branch networks, with statutory compliance.
  • Procurement and supplier management with segregation of duties.
  • Fixed assets across branches, from ATMs to vehicles, with depreciation and verification.
  • Branch expense control against budget, with approval hierarchies.
  • Management reporting that consolidates the core system's GL extract with operating costs.
  • Suitable for banks, SACCOs, microfinance institutions and insurers.
Energy & Utilities

Utilities Edition

For generation, transmission, distribution and water utilities where the balance sheet is made of assets in the field and the workforce that keeps them running.

  • Asset registers by substation, plant, line and zone, with condition and history.
  • Preventive and breakdown maintenance with work orders, crews and spares.
  • Spares and consumables inventory across depots, with reorder rules.
  • Field workforce scheduling, attendance and payroll.
  • Project capitalisation from work‑in‑progress to fixed asset.
  • Regulatory and board reporting from one governed data set.
What an edition contains

Configuration, not code – so it stays upgradeable.

Editions are delivered as ERPNext configuration and RailGrid apps that follow the Frappe framework's rules, so standard version upgrades continue to apply.

Workflows

Approval chains, document states and delegated authority matching how the sector is regulated.

Master data

Chart of accounts, cost centres, item groups, HR structures and templates ready for the sector.

Reports & dashboards

The KPIs and statutory reports the sector's executives and regulators actually ask for.

Controls

Role sets, segregation of duties and audit configurations that pass an internal‑audit review.
