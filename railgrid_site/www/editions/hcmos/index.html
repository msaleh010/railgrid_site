Core capabilities

One system. The full employee lifecycle.

Every module is standard ERPNext HR and Payroll, configured for multi‑site operations and Tanzanian statutory rules.

Lifecycle management

Onboarding, transfers, role changes and exits – every employee event captured once, with full audit trail and document checklist.

Attendance & leave

Biometric and geofenced attendance, shift rosters for camps and sites, leave entitlements and approval chains feeding straight into payroll.

Payroll & statutory

PAYE, NSSF, WCF, SDL, HESLB and loan deductions; payslips, bank files and the monthly returns – posted to your finance system or run in ERPNext accounting.

Performance & appraisals

Objective tracking, structured appraisal cycles and competency‑based reviews tied to training and progression.

Training & compliance

Training records, certification expiry tracking and enforcement of the licences and inductions a site requires before someone works.

Grievances & governance

Grievance cases with escalation and case tracking, role‑based access, override audit logs and segregation of duties across HR functions.

ESS field app

The institution's HR system, in every employee's pocket.

Employee self‑service on Android and iOS, built on the ERPNext mobile experience. Staff in the field do their own HR admin; HR stops re‑keying.

  • Digital payslips with download history.
  • Real‑time leave balances and applications.
  • Geofenced attendance check‑in from the field.
  • Document submission and acknowledgement.
  • Grievance escalation with case tracking.
  • Profile and statutory record updates, approved by HR.
